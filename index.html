FAQ

Questions we hear.

Still have questions? Call us at (904) 814-7544 — we'll talk it through.

How much does landscaping cost in St. Augustine FL?
Landscaping costs in St. Augustine typically range from $1,500 for basic lawn care and cleanup to $15,000+ for complete redesign and installation. Hardscaping projects usually start around $3,000. Every property is different — we offer free, no-obligation quotes.
Do you offer hurricane preparation services?
Yes. As a Florida-based company, hurricane prep is one of our specialties. We help St. Augustine homeowners and businesses secure outdoor items, trim hazardous branches, and prep landscaping ahead of storms. We also handle post-storm cleanup and debris removal.
What landscaping services do you offer?
Full-service landscaping including landscape design, lawn maintenance, sod installation, mulching, planting, irrigation systems, hardscaping (patios, walkways, retaining walls, fire pits), tree service, and hurricane preparation.
Do you serve areas outside St. Augustine?
Yes — St. Augustine is our home base but we serve surrounding Northeast Florida communities including St. Augustine Beach, Ponte Vedra, Vilano Beach, Crescent Beach, and nearby areas. Call us at (904) 814-7544 to confirm your address.
Are you licensed and insured?
Yes. We're fully licensed, insured, and bonded to operate in Florida. We're happy to provide proof on request — and always recommend hiring landscapers in St. Augustine who can do the same.
How quickly can you start a new project?
For lawn care and maintenance, usually within a week. Larger landscape design or hardscaping projects typically begin 2–4 weeks after consultation, depending on permits and materials. Storm cleanup is prioritized.
